   - Search terms: "german classroom", "visa documents", "nursing equipment"

2. **Pexels** (https://pexels.com)

3. **Pixabay** (https://pixabay.com)

## Image Specifications

### Technical Requirements:
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 500px × 500px minimum (square)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square) works best
- **File Size**: Under 500KB for fast loading
- **Quality**: High resolution, clear and professional

### Visual Requirements:
- **Bright and clear** images
- **Professional** appearance
- **Relevant** to the service
- **No faces** (or faces from behind/side)
- **Good lighting**
- **Clean background**

## How to Implement

### Step 1: Download Photos
Download 6 photos (one for each service) from stock photo websites.

### Step 2: Rename Files
Save them in the `Img` folder with these names:
```

Img/service-visa.jpg         → Student Visa Support
Img/service-culture.jpg      → Cultural Integration
Img/service-nursing.jpg      → Nursing Preparation
Img/service-hotel.jpg        → Hotel Service Training
```

## Tips for Choosing Photos

✅ **DO:**
- Choose bright, professional photos
- Use images that clearly represent the service
- Select high-quality, clear images
- Ensure photos have good lighting
- Pick images with simple, clean backgrounds

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use blurry or low-quality images
- Choose photos with too much text
- Select images with distracting backgrounds
- Use photos with visible faces (unless from behind)
- Pick images that are too dark or unclear
